# Copyright Preparation Checklist for the Software/App

This is a project-management checklist, not legal advice.

## Before filing

- Confirm the exact title of the software work.
- Identify each human author who wrote source code, UI copy, graphics, documentation, and original datasets.
- Identify the copyright owner/claimant and document assignments from authors when ownership differs.
- Remove third-party code, government content, logos, model weights, screenshots, and datasets from the deposit unless licensed and appropriate to include.
- Preserve Git commits, dated notebook versions, design files, and contributor agreements.
- Create a dependency and open-source license inventory.
- Decide whether the work is published or unpublished and record the first-publication date/country if applicable.
- Prepare a clean source-code deposit. The Indian Copyright Office currently describes software registration under literary works and requests source-code material under its rules and filing instructions.
- Keep the title, author, owner, publication status, and dates consistent across the application, source header, README, and deposit.

## Suggested source header

```text
Copyright (c) 2026. All rights reserved.
Sahayak AI Scheme Advisor — multilingual government-scheme discovery software.
Third-party libraries and government information remain the property of their respective owners.
```

## Official references to review at filing time

- India Copyright Office / IP India online services and current migration notices
- Current Form XIV and Statement of Particulars
- Current software-deposit instructions
- Current fee schedule and payment workflow

Because government filing portals and instructions change, verify all requirements on the official site immediately before submission.
