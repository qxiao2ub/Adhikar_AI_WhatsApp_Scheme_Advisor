# Impact metric definitions

- **Users reached:** cumulative non-identifying app-session counter.
- **Consented analytics profiles:** pseudonymous profiles that opted into impact analytics.
- **Scheme matches generated:** scheme-match records saved for consented profiles.
- **Potential beneficiaries identified:** unique consented profiles with at least one Potential match or Needs official review result.
- **Application journeys initiated:** unique consented profiles that clicked Start application journey.
- **Self-reported completions:** unique consented profiles that clicked Mark completed.
- **Geography/language/need category:** aggregate counts from privacy-minimized profiles; small groups are suppressed.

These are product and journey metrics. They do **not** prove official eligibility, approval, enrollment, payment, or benefits received. Measuring verified beneficiary outcomes would require an authorized integration or a documented verification process.
